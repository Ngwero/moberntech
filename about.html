About Mobenertech

We are a Ugandan technology company on a mission to be the best gateway to digital technologies for citizens, businesses and institutions worldwide.

Uganda & the world

Born in Kampala. Built for global scale.

Mobenertech Ltd was founded in Uganda to prove that world-class software, cloud platforms and digital products can be designed and delivered from East Africa. Our headquarters and primary engineering campus sit in Kampala — with delivery partnerships spanning the continent and overseas.

We serve banks, telcos, governments, NGOs and high-growth startups. Local context makes our products resilient; global standards make them ready for any market.
